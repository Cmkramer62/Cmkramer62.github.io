That's Not a Fire Extinguisher!

Built for a recent game jam, this game is a short 2D platformer. The main character is a lantern brought to life, who must extinguish fires throughout the kingdom.

UnityC#WebGL2D PlatformerGame Jam
Play on itch.io ↗

Project Overview

This game was built for UDC Jam #38 - Wrong Tool for the Job. The theme being the "wrong tool" in some way, the main character is a lantern brought to life. His master, the wizard, missed while trying to bring a fire extinguisher to life.

My Role

I built out the main gameplay systems, integrated audio packages and music, worked on UI/UX, and worked on the mini story/script.

Design & Development

Developing this game was very fun. I kept everything small and concise, spending much time building out new levels after I got all gameplay systems working.

Challenges & Outcomes

The time was the greatest challenge I faced. It forced me to think differently about the art style, how much I was okay with learning, and how ambitious I got around gameplay mechanics and world-building.
