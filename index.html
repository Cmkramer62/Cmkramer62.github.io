05 — A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Making Games

I’m Cameron, thanks for checking out my portfolio! Here's a little more about myself.

I was born and raised in California, and grew up playing videogames from a young age. Much of the early games I played were Nintendo classics, such as Pokemon Crystal, Super Mario World, and Animal Crossing. As I got older I played games like Minecraft, 7 Days to Die, and Modnation Racers. All games that allow users to create things. In combination with hobbies such as drawing and arts & crafts I realized that my passion lied in the intersection between games and creation. As soon as I realized that, I started working on my own games.

I have a soft spot for fantasy, horror, and sci-fi genres. I also especially the multiplayer and MMO field. Also, despite loving games with creative outlets for their users, my favorite game of all time is Portal 2!
